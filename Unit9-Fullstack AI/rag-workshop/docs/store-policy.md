# Returns

Trailhead Outfitters accepts returns within 45 days of delivery for unused items in their original packaging. Refunds go to the original payment method within 7 business days. Items marked "Final Sale" cannot be returned.

# Shipping

Standard shipping takes 5-7 business days and is free on orders over $75, otherwise $5.99. Express shipping takes 2 business days and costs $14.99. We currently ship to the US and Canada only.

# Warranty

Ridgeline tents have a 5 year warranty against manufacturing defects. Alpine steel water bottles have a lifetime warranty.
