Q: What payment methods do you accept?
A: We accept Visa, Mastercard, American Express, and PayPal. We do not accept checks or cash on delivery.

Q: How do I track my order?
A: Once your order ships, you'll receive an email with a tracking number. You can also check order status by logging into your account and going to "My Orders."

Q: Can I change my shipping address after placing an order?
A: Only within 1 hour of placing the order. After that, the order has already been sent to our warehouse and the address cannot be changed.

Q: Do you offer gift wrapping?
A: Yes, gift wrapping is available at checkout for a flat fee of $4.50 per item.

Q: What if my item arrives damaged?
A: Contact support within 14 days of delivery with a photo of the damage, and we'll send a free replacement or a full refund.

Q: Do you have a loyalty or rewards program?
A: Yes. You earn 1 point per dollar spent, and 100 points can be redeemed for a $5 discount on a future order.
